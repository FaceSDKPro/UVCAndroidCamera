UVC Dual Camera Privacy Policy

Developer: HelloFace · Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy applies to UVC Dual Camera (com.helloface.uvccamera), an Android tool for previewing and configuring two independent USB cameras.

Camera access and data use

When you start capture, Android requests camera permission and access to the selected USB devices. The app reads device information, such as product names and USB identifiers, to list and select cameras. Video frames are processed in memory for preview and frame pairing. The app does not record, save or upload frames.

Storage, sharing and deletion

The app has no accounts, ads or analytics. It does not send camera frames or USB device details off the device or share them with third parties. Frame buffers are released when capture ends. Android may restore camera selections and capture settings when it recreates a screen. Clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove its local app state. Camera permission can be revoked in Android Settings.

The app displays this policy on the device. If you visit the online policy, your browser and the website handle that visit separately from the app.

Email FaceAISDK.Service@gmail.com for questions about this policy or the app. If you email us, we use the information you provide to respond and keep it only as needed for the request or applicable obligations.

UVC 双目相机隐私政策

开发者：HelloFace · 更新日期：2026 年 11 月 6 日

本政策适用于 UVC 双目相机（com.helloface.uvccamera）。这是一款用于预览和配置两个独立 USB 摄像头的 Android 工具。

相机访问与数据用途

开始采集时，Android 会请求相机权限和访问所选 USB 设备的授权。应用读取产品名称、USB 标识等设备信息，用于列出和选择摄像头。视频帧在设备内存中处理，用于预览和帧配对。应用不会录制、保存或上传画面。

存储、共享与删除

应用没有账号、广告或分析统计功能。应用不会将相机帧或 USB 设备信息发送到设备外，也不会与第三方共享这些信息。停止采集后释放帧缓冲区。Android 重新创建界面时，可能恢复所选摄像头和采集参数。清除应用数据或卸载可移除本机应用状态，也可在 Android 设置中撤销相机权限。

应用在设备上显示本政策。如果您访问在线政策，浏览器和网站会独立处理该次访问。

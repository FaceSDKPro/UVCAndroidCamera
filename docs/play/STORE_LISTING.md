## English (United States)

**App name**

UVC Dual Camera

**Short description**

Preview and configure two USB cameras with independent RGB and IR streams.

**Full description**

UVC Dual Camera is a utility for previewing and configuring two independent USB video cameras on Android. Assign an RGB camera and an infrared camera, then view both streams or inspect paired frame statistics.

- Select the resolution, capture frame rate, transport format and rotation for each camera.
- Preview both cameras and adjust brightness on supported devices.
- Inspect live frame rates, frame pairs and dropped frame counts.

You need an Android device with USB host support and two separately detected UVC cameras. Use a compatible USB hub or adapter if needed. Available settings depend on the cameras and connection.

Camera frames are processed locally for live preview. The app does not record video or save images. Frame pairing uses arrival times and does not synchronize the cameras' exposures.

**Release notes**

Preview and configure separate RGB and IR USB cameras. The interface follows the device language and supports English and Simplified Chinese.

## 简体中文

**应用名称**

UVC 双目相机

**简短描述**

预览和配置两个独立 USB 摄像头，查看 RGB 与红外画面及帧对统计。

**详细描述**

UVC 双目相机是一款在 Android 上预览和配置两个独立 USB 摄像头的工具。分别选择 RGB 可见光和 IR 红外摄像头，即可查看双路画面或检查帧对统计。

- 每路可单独设置分辨率、采集帧率、传输格式及旋转角度。
- 支持双路预览，并可在设备支持时调节亮度。
- 查看实时帧率、帧对和丢弃帧数。

需要支持 USB Host 的 Android 设备及两个能被系统独立识别的 UVC 摄像头，必要时使用兼容的 USB 集线器或转接器。可用参数以摄像头和连接条件为准。

相机画面在本机处理，用于实时预览。应用不录像或保存图像。软件按帧到达时间配对，无法同步两路摄像头的曝光。

**版本说明**

支持独立 RGB 和 IR USB 摄像头的预览与配置。界面支持英文和简体中文，随系统语言显示。
